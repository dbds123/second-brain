# What if #1: "What if your money doubled every second?" (script v1)

~41 s, voice Titan, loop. The counter is REAL TIME: it shows 2^t coins, t = seconds since frame 1, timer chip "T+ns" next to it.
Key lines are placed in the mix so the spoken number lands when the counter shows it.

Math (coin = 7.5 g like a 1-euro coin, loose pile 1.65 cm³ per coin):
10 s = 1,024 coins = 7.7 kg | 17 s = 131,072 = 0.98 t | 20 s = 1,048,576 = 7.9 t (African elephant ~6 t)
25 s = 33.5 M = 252 t, 55 m³ (buries a small hut) | 30 s = 1.07 B = 8,050 t, 1,770 m³ (a ~10 m hill)
38 s = 275 B = 2.06 M t.
