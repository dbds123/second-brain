# 07 – One Coconut of Land (v2, 2026-11-19 evening)

**v2 replaces v1 (skript-v1.md).** Daniel 19.11.: v1 had no dramaturgy, no hook, a static camera and one single scene. v2 is built on the grammar of the channel's best short ("He traded diamonds for ONE cup of water", 58 shots in 55 s): paradox hook that shows the deal first, a villain and an underdog, a time arc with day markers, the whole island laughs, the roles flip, a turn after the climax, loop.

Format: Island Deals fable · seamless loop · narrator "Titan" (excited) · one-word captions · about 52 s · about 50 shots
Cast: Baron Goldbelly (landlord, the villain), Squid (underdog, then the new landlord), crabs and Chad Fisher as tenants.
Story in three sentences: The Baron owns every grain of sand and charges rent for sitting, shade and sleep, and he doubles Squid's rent every day until Squid has one coin left. For that coin the Baron mockingly sells him "all the land you can fence with one coconut", and overnight Squid spins the husk into one thread that goes around the whole island. Now the Baron pays the rent, and Squid doubles it every day until the Baron is the one with a last coin.

Build note: it is a thin coconut-fibre THREAD on small sticks with red flags, cream-yellow and unlit so it reads at phone size. Never a rope, never the word rope.

## Characters and what they feel (play these traits in every shot they are in)

- **Baron Goldbelly:** the villain. Vain, greedy, bored by his own wealth, he squeezes the island because he can and he enjoys watching others count coins. He never walks, he is carried. He laughs at his own jokes with his head thrown back. His fall must hurt and be funny: nightcap, dropped cup, shaking wing, paying to sit on sand that was his.
- **Squid:** the underdog. Stoic, proud, never begs and never shows a feeling behind his sunglasses. His stand is all he has: a crooked little stall he built himself, with a hand-painted sign "SQUID'S". The audience must fear for that stand. Emotional beats: shot 16 (the last coin, and behind him two of the Baron's crabs nail a board "EVICTED TOMORROW" over his sign) and shot 27 (alone at night by a candle, he straightens the crooked sign with one tentacle before he starts to peel). He wins with patience and work, not with a trick out of nowhere.
- **Crabs and Chad:** tired tenants. They laugh at Squid because laughing is free, and they cheer quietly in shot 40 when the Baron has to pay.

## Direction rules for this short (they matter more than any single shot)

- About one shot per second. Never hold a framing longer than 2 s except shots 16 and 27 (the two emotional beats) and 38 (the sigma beat).
- No static camera, ever: push-in, crash zoom, whip, crane, low tracking, orbit, drone.
- Every sentence gets its own picture and its own place. Six places: the land desk, the beach with tenants, Squid's stand, Squid's stand at night under the moon, the village, the mansion on the hill.
- Three looks: hard hot daylight for the Baron's rule, deep blue moonlight for the night, golden sunrise for the reveal. After the flip the daylight is the same hot look as at the start, only with Squid on the throne.
- Day cards are big wooden calendar boards that slam into frame ("DAY 3", "1 MONTH LATER"), 0.6 s each, with camera shake.
- The rent tag is a running prop: a wooden flip counter on a post. It is the meter of the story and must be readable every time.
- No doubling or ghost copy of any character, only a soft contact shadow.
- Characters and props always move: anticipation, overshoot, squash and stretch, coins bounce, tags swing.
- Review rules as before: heads large, tags readable and never cropped, captions never on a sign, tag, face or hero object.

## Frame 1 (also the last frame)

ECU on the land desk from slightly below: ONE brown coconut (fibre lines, three dots) and ONE coin that is just being slammed down next to it, bold wooden tag between them: "LAND · 1 COIN". Behind them, left and right, both faces large: the Baron and Squid nose to beak over the desk, heads at least 25 % of frame height each. Which one is selling is not readable from the picture, that is the point of the loop.
