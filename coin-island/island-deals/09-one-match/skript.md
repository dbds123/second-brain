# 09 – One Match (2026-11-23)

Format: Island Deals fable · seamless loop · narrator "Titan" (excited) · one-word captions · about 56 s · about 52 shots
Built on the diamond grammar and on what the v2 batch taught us: the first sentence is cut on its words (no picture longer than about 1 s in the first 4.5 s), frame 0 shows the thing, its tag and the shocked face at once.

Story in three sentences: In the heat the Baron sells a box of matches as junk for one coin, and the whole island laughs at Hedgie for buying fire on a tropical island. Then it snows, the sea freezes, and the Baron sits in a mansion full of wood with nothing to light it, while the only flame on the island is Hedgie's. The Baron pays his yacht for ONE match, a snowflake puts it out, and the next morning it is forty degrees again.

Why this one: the channel's best short and the best v2 starts are all "everything for a survival item" (diamonds for water, the rock that opens coconuts). Here the item is fire, the protagonist is new for an Island Deal (Hedgie), and the world flips visibly (a tropical island under snow), so it reads with the sound off.

## Characters and what they feel (play these traits in every shot they are in)

- **Hedgie:** hopeful gambler who always buys at the wrong time. He spends his last coin on matches in the heat and believes in it for about one second, until everybody laughs. Shot 13 is his low: spines droop from green to red, sunglasses slip, one sad eye. From shot 30 he is calm and warm, paws at his little fire, spines green. He never gloats; the flame in his sunglasses does it for him.
- **Baron Goldbelly:** vain, greedy, calls everyone "peasant", never carries anything himself. He sells the matches because he finds them worthless and enjoys the insult. His fall is the fun: blue with cold (use the blue Baron variant), an icicle on his beak, on his knees, hat off, "Please… sir." Then he loses the match too.
- **Chad Fisher:** huge, calm, notices nothing. Fishes in the heat, fishes through a hole in the ice, a snowflake lands on his jaw and he does not blink.
- **Crabs:** the crowd. They laugh first and freeze first (one block of ice with eyes).

## Direction rules for this short

- About one shot per second. Never hold a framing longer than 2 s except shots 13 and 44 (the two emotional beats).
- No static camera. Fast action is tracked by the camera so figures stay sharp; no motion blur on figure cards; nobody disappears before a cut.
- FRAMING RULE and caption safe zone as in the v2 batch (subject fills 50 % of height or 70 % of width, bare ground max 20 %, sky max 25 %, no cut faces, no slivers, tags whole, sharp and facing the camera, captions 18 to 72 % and never on a face, tag or hero object).
- Snow: flakes fall BEHIND the figures or small and sparse in the depth. No flake close to the lens and none across a face, except the two scripted single flakes (shots 17 and 44), which are big, slow and alone in the frame.
- Looks: Monday is hot, saturated orange with heat shimmer. From Tuesday the island turns white and blue, breath clouds, long shadows. Hedgie's fire is the only warm colour in the cold world (the match is the only orange thing, like the rock was the only grey thing). Friday snaps back to the hot look in one frame.
- Characters: untouched original art only. The cold is shown with the existing blue Baron variant, shivering, breath clouds and icicle props, not with a new shading pass.
- Day boards slam in, 0.5 s each, with shake; they stay inside the frame.
