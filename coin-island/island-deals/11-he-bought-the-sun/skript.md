# 11 – He Bought the Sun (2026-11-25)

Format: Island Deals fable · HARD seamless loop · narrator "Titan" (excited) · one-word captions · UNDER 30 s · about 27 shots

THIS SHORT IS A LENGTH TEST. The channel's two breakouts (Diamonds 20k, the loop short 11.5k) were both under 35 s, the last shorts are 48 to 63 s and sit at 1.2 to 1.5k. So this one is under 30 s with a loop that cannot be told from a cut. It keeps the calm opening of 10 (plain sky and sand, one subject per shot, no tag or sign in the first sentence, daylight palette, no orange grade).

Story in three sentences: The Baron buys the sun from Squid for all his gold and wants to charge the island one coin a minute for sunshine. Nobody pays, everybody just moves into the shade, and in the evening his sun goes down and Squid tells him it now belongs to somebody on the other side. He sits broke in the dark all night, and at sunrise Squid is back with a new deal.

## Characters and what they feel

- **Baron Goldbelly:** greedy and proud. Glowing with pride at the start (he owns the sun), furious when nobody pays, panic when it sets, tiny and broke in the dark, and greedy again in the last shot. That last look is the loop.
- **Squid:** quiet, never argues, only states the terms. Sunglasses.
- **Crabs, Chad:** the island. They do not fight him, they simply stop needing his sun.

1. Opening (the whole first sentence, about 0 to 3 s): only plain blue sky and pale sand behind one subject per shot. No tag, sign, stall, palm, yacht, aerial or wide shot. Cut on the words, no picture longer than about 0.8 s.
2. Daylight palette and light exactly as in 10, soft bloom only. Night (shots 19 to 22) is deep blue with stars and a small cold moon, figures stay readable, no black frame.
