# 06 – It Rained Gold (v2, 2026-11-19 evening)

**v2 replaces v1 (skript-v1.md).** Daniel 19.11.: the story of v1 is bad; he wants real stories that wake emotions, with characters who have traits. v2 is built on the grammar of the channel's best short ("He traded diamonds for ONE cup of water", 58 shots in 55 s): paradox hook, a time arc with day markers, the island laughs at one of them, the world changes, the roles flip, a turn after the climax, loop.

Format: Island Deals fable · seamless loop · narrator "Titan" (excited) · one-word captions · about 55 s · about 52 shots
Story in three sentences: It rains gold on everyone except Squid, who gets one rock, and the Baron calls him a peasant while the island laughs. Because everybody is rich, nobody works, a fish costs a wheelbarrow of gold, and in the end there is only hard coconuts and nothing on the island hard enough to open them, except the rock. The hungry millionaires line up, the Baron has to say "sir", and then it rains rocks.

## Characters and what they feel (play these traits in every shot they are in)

- **Squid:** stoic, patient, never shows a feeling behind his sunglasses. The one time something gets through is shot 12, when the rock hits him and everybody laughs: his glasses sit crooked and one sad eye shows for a second. The audience must feel for him there.
- **Baron Goldbelly:** vain and greedy, calls everyone "peasant", holds his top hat like a crown. He takes the most gold and shoves smaller animals aside. His fall is the fun: he ends on his knees and has to say "sir".
- **Chad Fisher:** huge, calm, notices nothing. A gold bar bounces off his jaw and he does not blink. He simply stops fishing because he is rich now.
- **Crabs:** the crowd. They laugh first and crawl first.

## Direction rules for this short (they matter more than any single shot)

- About one shot per second. Never hold a framing longer than 2 s except shots 12 and 39 (the two emotional beats).
- No static camera, ever: push-in, crash zoom, whip, crane, low tracking, orbit.
- Every sentence gets its own picture. Places: the beach in the gold rain, the Baron's golden hoard, Chad's boat at the pier, the fish stand, the bakery hut, the coconut grove, Squid's corner of the beach, the lagoon with the yacht.
- Looks: golden glare for the rain days, a tired washed-out yellow when everybody is hungry (gold everywhere and nobody cares), cold grey for the rock rain.
- Day cards are big wooden calendar boards that slam into frame, 0.5 s each, with camera shake. In the price run (shots 20 to 25) they come fast like a drum beat.
- The rock is plain grey with crack lines, unlit, and it is the only grey thing in a yellow world.
- No doubling or ghost copy of any character, only a soft contact shadow.
- Characters and props always move: anticipation, overshoot, squash and stretch, bars bounce, tags swing.
- Review rules as before: heads large, tags readable and never cropped, captions never on a sign, tag, face or hero object.

## Frame 1 (also the last frame)

Low angle on the beach: the sky is bright yellow and the first gold bars fall toward the camera, one of them huge in the foreground. In the centre Squid, looking up, head at least 25 % of frame height, a single grey rock just above his head on its way down. Left of him the Baron with his top hat held open like a bucket, greedy grin. Wooden tag stuck in the sand: "FREE GOLD".
