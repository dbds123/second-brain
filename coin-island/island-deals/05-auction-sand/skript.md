# 05 – The Auction (v2, 2026-11-19 evening)

**v2 replaces v1 (skript-v1.md).** Daniel 19.11.: the v1 stories have no dramaturgy and no emotion; he wants real stories with characters who have traits. v2 is built on the grammar of the channel's best short ("He traded diamonds for ONE cup of water", 58 shots in 55 s): paradox hook that shows the end first, a villain and someone to feel for, a time arc with day markers, the roles flip, a turn after the climax, loop.

Format: Island Deals fable · seamless loop · narrator "Titan" (excited) · one-word captions · about 56 s · about 52 shots
Cast: Baron Goldbelly (the villain), a little crab (the heart of the story), Toko (toucan auctioneer, r3 design as built), Squid (silent avenger, sigma moment), crabs and Chad Fisher as the crowd.
Story in three sentences: The Baron goes to every auction and outbids poor animals out of spite, and this Sunday he takes an old shell from a little crab who saved all year to buy back his grandmother's home. But at Toko's auction every move is a bid, and when lot seven is one worthless grain of sand, Squid opens the pepper and the Baron sneezes away his yacht, his mansion and his hat. The next Sunday everything he owned is auctioned, the little crab gets the shell for three coins because nobody bids against him, and the Baron sits on his wings until his beak starts to itch.

## Characters and what they feel (play these traits in every shot they are in)

- **Baron Goldbelly:** the villain. Vain and greedy. He does not come to auctions to buy, he comes to be seen winning. He only wants a thing when someone else wants it, and he loses interest the second it is his: he tosses his wins over his shoulder onto a pile. He bids with one lazy feather and does not even look. His fall must be funny and deserved: he fights his own body (itch, fly, sneeze) and loses.
- **The little crab:** small, round eyes, a tiny patched shell that is too small for him, a clay piggy bank with exactly three coins. He does not speak much and he never whines. The audience must love him in two seconds (shot 13) and hurt for him in shot 19. He is why the sneeze feels like justice and not like a random gag.
- **Toko:** strict, fast, fair. The rule is the rule, for the poor and for the rich. He takes no side, his eyes only hunt for movement. His beak is the hammer. The one time he is gentle is the quiet "Sold." in shot 47.
- **Squid:** stoic and silent, sits in the last row. He sees everything and shows nothing. In shot 20 the crying crab is reflected in his sunglasses: that is his motive. He acts once, with one twist of a pepper mill.
- **Crowd (crabs, Chad):** afraid of the Baron. Nobody dares to bid against him. In shot 46 they all sit on their claws on purpose.

## Direction rules for this short (they matter more than any single shot)

- About one shot per second. Never hold a framing longer than 2 s except shots 19, 20 (the sad beat), 30, 31 (the freeze) and 47, 48 (the quiet win).
- No static camera, ever: push-in, crash zoom, whip, crane, low tracking, orbit. Impacts (beak on the block, sneeze) shake the camera.
- Every sentence gets its own picture. Places: the auction tent (stage side and crowd side are two different views), the Baron's trophy pile behind his seat, the little crab's burrow, the lagoon with the yacht, the mansion on the hill, the beach outside the tent.
- Looks: warm red and gold tent light for the Baron's Sunday; cold blue for the crab's loss (shots 19, 20, the tent light itself seems to dim around him); hard white spotlight on the Baron for the freeze; soft golden evening light for the second Sunday.
- Day cards are big wooden calendar boards that slam into frame ("SUNDAY", "NEXT SUNDAY"), 0.5 s each, with camera shake. Lot cards are Toko's flip tag on the block ("LOT 6", "LOT 7", "LOT 8"); the bids flip on the same tag.
- Toko's beak hitting the block is the drum of this short: same dry "tok" every time, the block jumps, dust rises.
- No doubling or ghost copy of any character, only a soft contact shadow. Unused pose cards are parked out of frame (the black Toko fault of r2 must not come back).
- Characters and props always move: anticipation, overshoot, squash and stretch, tags swing, feathers fly.
- Review rules as before: heads large, tags readable and never cropped, captions never on a sign, tag, face or hero object.
