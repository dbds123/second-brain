# 08 – Buy the Dip (v2, 2026-11-19 evening)

**v2 replaces v1 (skript-v1.md).** Daniel 19.11.: v1 had no dramaturgy, no hook, a static camera and one single scene. v2 is built on the grammar of the channel's best short ("He traded diamonds for ONE cup of water", 58 shots in 55 s): paradox hook that shows the end first, a time arc with visible day markers, the whole island laughs, the world changes, the roles flip, a turn after the climax, loop.

Format: Idiom Island fable · seamless loop · narrator "Titan" (excited) · one-word captions · about 55 s · about 52 shots
Cast: Hedgie (approved v4b side design), crab chorus (3 crabs), Squid (dip seller), Chad Fisher and the Baron as crowd. Seagulls.
Story in three sentences: The crabs scream "buy the dip" about crashing coconut prices, Hedgie takes it literally and trades his house for forty bowls of guacamole, and the island laughs. Then a shipwreck floods the beach with a million nacho chips, and the only dip on the island belongs to him. The next day the chips are gone, and the day the crabs scream again he is already running.

## Characters and what they feel (play these traits in every shot they are in)

- **Hedgie:** the island's gambler. He believes every hype, hears only what he wants to hear and is always all in, always at the wrong moment. Under the shades and the gold chain he is a soft, hopeful little guy, and that is why the audience roots for him. The one time it shows is shot 21b: alone in the rain in front of the house that was his, hugging a bowl of dip. The audience must feel sorry for him there, so that the turn on Tuesday feels like justice.
- **Crab chorus:** loud know-it-alls who shout the same tip every week and never buy themselves. They laugh the loudest on Monday and crawl first on Tuesday.
- **Squid:** stoic dealer. Sells anything to anyone, never blinks, counts the key with a grin. On Tuesday even he has to crawl and give up his sunglasses.
- **Baron Goldbelly:** vain and greedy, laughs at Hedgie from above with his wing in front of his beak. Losing his top hat in shot 37 is his small humiliation.
- **Chad Fisher:** huge, calm, notices nothing. Laughs one slow "ha".

## Direction rules for this short (they matter more than any single shot)

- About one shot per second. Never hold a framing longer than 2 s except shots 21b and 21c (the sad night) and 35 and 36 (the sigma beat).
- No static camera, ever: push-in, crash zoom, whip, crane, low tracking, orbit. Hard cuts on the beat, whips only where marked.
- Every sentence gets its own picture and its own place. Seven places: Squid's dip stand, the coconut market, Hedgie's hut with his boat, the hut at night in the rain, the storm sea, the chip beach, Hedgie's gold hill.
- Three looks, switched hard with the day cards: Monday warm afternoon, Tuesday storm blue then bright white-yellow chip beach, Wednesday grey morning. The last shots return to Monday's warm look.
- Day cards are big wooden calendar boards that slam into frame ("MONDAY", "TUESDAY", "WEDNESDAY", "1 WEEK LATER"), 0.6 s each, with camera shake.
- No doubling or ghost copy of any character, only a soft contact shadow.
- Characters and props always move: anticipation, overshoot, squash and stretch, bowls wobble, tags swing.
- Review rules as before: heads large, tags readable and never cropped, captions never on a sign, tag, face or hero object.
