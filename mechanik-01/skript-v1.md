# Mechanik-Short 01: "Peek before you flip" (Münzwurf, ~40 s)

Stil: Skill hidden-mechanism-short. Sprecher: Titan (dtSEyYGNJqjrtBArPCVZ). Figuren: Rico (wirft), Squid (lacht, verliert), Desmond (macht es absichtlich).
Fakt dahinter: Ein geworfener Münze eiert (Präzession) und landet etwa 51 % auf der Seite, die oben lag (Diaconis 2007; 350.757 Würfe, 50,8 %, Bartoš et al. 2023).

Titel-Ideen: "Why you should peek before you flip" / "The coin flip isn't 50/50"

## Sprechertext (124 Wörter)
If you peek at a coin before you flip it, you'll win more bets than anyone on the island.
See. Rico holds the coin. Heads up. He flips… and calls heads. Every time.
Squid laughs. Now, here's the weird part. Rico keeps winning.
Because a flipped coin doesn't just spin. It wobbles.
And while it wobbles, the side that started on top stays facing the sky a little longer.
So it lands the way it started… fifty-one percent of the time.
"One percent?" says Squid. "That's nothing."
But Desmond flips a thousand times a day. On purpose.
That's ten extra wins. Every. Single. Day.
Bomba– *BEEP*
Tiny edge, huge pile. That's the house edge.
So next time, before you flip… peek.

8 Auflösung: kleiner Vorteil, riesiger Haufen
9 Callback "peek" + letztes Bild = erstes Bild (Rico spickt auf die Münze) → Loop in "If you peek…"
Payoff-Objekt früh versteckt: Desmonds Eimer voller Münzen steht ab Shot 2 im Hintergrund.
